Why Ever Gotesco is My Favorite Place

Ever Gotesco is filled with countless simple yet unforgettable memories—especially hanging out after classes to play at Tom's World and shooting a few rounds of billiards with friends. Whether it's competing for high scores on arcade games, trying to win prizes, or relaxing over a game of pool, it’s always been our go-to spot to unwind. Having a place along Commonwealth where you can grab a quick snack, chill, and just have fun without needing to travel far makes it a staple part of daily student life that always feels welcoming.
